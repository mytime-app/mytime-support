Overview

MyTime is an independently developed companion application for MyKronoz ZeTime watches. MyTime is not affiliated with or endorsed by MyKronoz.

MyTime does not sell personal information, display targeted advertising, or use advertising trackers or developer-operated behavioral analytics.

Information stored on your device

MyTime may store the following information locally:

  • ZeTime identifiers and connection preferences
  • Activity, sleep, and heart-rate records
  • Battery readings and supported watch settings
  • Goals and synchronization preferences
  • Saved watch-face images and identifiers
  • Music-service preferences
  • Spotify authorization credentials

Spotify credentials are protected using the iOS Keychain. MyTime does not operate a developer-controlled server that receives your watch or health records.

Bluetooth

MyTime uses Bluetooth to pair and reconnect your ZeTime, synchronize supported data, change watch settings, provide music controls, and transfer watch faces.

Information received from the watch remains on your device unless this policy specifically describes a different use.

Weather and location

When weather synchronization is used, MyTime requests your device location at the precision permitted by your iOS settings. The resulting coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve current weather and forecast information. Apple services may also be used to determine a readable city name.

Activity, sleep, heart-rate, contact, and watch-identifying information are not included in weather requests. You can revoke location permission in iOS Settings, although weather synchronization may then stop working.

Music services

Spotify

If you select Spotify, MyTime communicates directly with Spotify to authenticate your account, retrieve current playback information and album artwork, and issue supported playback commands. MyTime does not receive or store your Spotify password. You can remove locally stored Spotify credentials by selecting Log Out in the app.

Apple Music

If you select Apple Music, MyTime uses Apple system media APIs to read and control supported playback information. MyTime does not upload your Apple Music library or listening history to the developer.

Watch-face photos

Photos selected for custom watch faces are processed locally and transferred directly to the connected ZeTime using Bluetooth. Saved watch-face images remain in the app's local storage until you remove them or delete the app.

Feedback and bug reports

Choosing Feedback or Support opens an external Google Form. Google and the developer receive only the information you choose to submit. Submitted information may be retained as reasonably necessary to investigate the report, communicate with you, and improve MyTime.

Data retention and deletion

Activity, sleep, heart-rate, watch-face, and preference data remain on your device until you delete them using MyTime's controls or remove the app.

Spotify credentials should be removed using Log Out before deleting MyTime because iOS Keychain data may persist after an app is removed. Bluetooth, location, photo, and media permissions can be revoked through iOS Settings.

To request deletion of information voluntarily submitted through the feedback form, contact the developer using the address below.

Children's privacy

MyTime is not directed specifically toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

MyTime may interact with these services:

  • Open-Meteo for weather information
  • Spotify for optional Spotify integration
  • Apple for system services and device permissions
  • Google Forms for optional feedback and bug reports

Use of these services is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as MyTime's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
